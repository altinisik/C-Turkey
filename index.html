How to cite. Altınışık, N.E. (2026) “C-Turkey: A Comprehensive Radiocarbon Dataset from Türkiye,” Journal of Open Archaeology Data, 14. doi:10.5334/joad.213. Please also cite the primary sources listed in the Reference column.

About the data. C-Turkey integrates records from global repositories (such as C14bazaar and AADR) with regional literature, national excavation reports and direct dates from ancient DNA studies. Downloads contain every column of the dataset for the rows that match your filters. Methodology and primary sources are described in the paper and the repository.
